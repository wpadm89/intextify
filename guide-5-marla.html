A Complete Guide to 5 Marla House Construction Cost in Lahore (2026)

By Adnan Akbar, Civil Engineer · Updated: May 2026

Building a 5 Marla house in Lahore is a dream for many, but without proper cost estimation and planning, budgets can quickly spiral out of control. Whether you are building in DHA, Bahria Town, or an LDA-approved society, understanding the current market rates for materials and labor is crucial.

Estimated Cost Summary (2026)

Based on current market conditions, here is a quick overview of what it costs to build a standard double-story 5 Marla house (approx. 1,900 to 2,100 sq. ft. covered area):

Construction Phase Estimated Cost (PKR)
Grey Structure 35 Lakh – 45 Lakh
Finishing (A-Category) 28 Lakh – 38 Lakh
Total Estimated Cost 63 Lakh – 83 Lakh

* Estimates based on May 2026 Lahore market rates. Actual costs vary by location, material grade, and contractor. Use our BOQ Estimator for a live, itemized calculation.

Phase 1: Grey Structure Breakdown

The grey structure forms the skeleton of your house. In 2026, costs heavily depend on the fluctuating rates of foundational materials:

  • Bricks (Awwal): 50,000 to 55,000 top-quality bricks required for a standard double-story unit.
  • Cement: 500 to 550 bags of standard Portland cement for masonry, plastering, and concrete slabs.
  • Sand (Ravi & Chenab): Chenab sand is preferred for roofing slabs; Ravi sand is used for regular brickwork.
  • Crush (Margalla & Sargodha): Essential for RCC lintels, roof slabs, and structural columns.
  • Steel (Sariya): 3 to 3.5 tons of 60-grade steel for structural integrity and seismic resistance.
  • Labor Cost: Contractor rates are charged per square foot of the total covered area.

Phase 2: Finishing Cost (Turnkey)

For an A-category finish, finishing costs typically amount to 75% to 85% of the grey structure cost:

  • Plumbing Works: UPVC/PPRC pipes for water supply and drainage, plus gas piping throughout.
  • Electrical Works: PVC conduits, copper wiring (Pakistan Cables or Fast Cables), distribution boards, and switches.
  • Wood, Metal & Tile Works: Aluminum windows, solid ash or oak wood doors, kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, and high-quality porcelain or ceramic tiles.
  • Fittings & Fixtures: Sanitary ware (commodes, vanities, shower sets) and electrical fixtures (lights, ceiling fans).

Tips to Optimize Your Construction Budget

1. Use Accurate Estimation Tools: Do not rely on guesswork. Use a digital BOQ Estimator to compute precise material quantities before purchasing anything.

2. Monitor Live Rates: Material prices in Pakistan fluctuate frequently. Buying steel or cement in bulk when rates dip can save hundreds of thousands of rupees.

3. Hire Reputable Contractors: A good contractor minimizes material wastage, saving money overall even if their per-sqft rate is slightly higher than the market average.
